## How to read this / important context

- 'Next' means the next event as of the 29 Oct 2026 check. Companies that already reported their Q3-season event on or before 28 Oct 2026 therefore show UNKNOWN here, with their last verified report date in Notes — their next (Q4-season, mostly Jan–Feb 2027) date had not been verified in the sources checked, except the four large banks where MarketBeat published a January 2027 estimate.
- Fiscal labels are each company's own (e.g. AAPL Q4 FY2026, MSFT Q1 FY2027, NVDA Q3 FY2027, QCOM Q4 FY2026, WMT Q3 FY2027, CSCO Q1 FY2027, CRM Q3 FY2027, ORCL Q2 FY2027). Fiscal quarters do **not** align with calendar quarters.
- Timing is BMO (before market open) / AMC (after market close) / UNKNOWN. Where a source gave only a morning call time, BMO is noted as inferred in Notes — check the release itself before relying on it.
- Weekday check: all dates were weekday-verified (e.g. 2 Nov 2026 is a Monday, 17 Nov a Tuesday, 19 Nov a Thursday).
